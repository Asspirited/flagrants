# Flagrants — Satirical Comedy Pattern Reference Guide & Orchestration Engine

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **CORE SATIRICAL LINEAGE & COMEDY DECOMPOSITION MANIFESTO**
> This reference guide codifies the comedic patterns derived from British satire (*Peter Cook, Spike Milligan, Tony Hancock, Monty Python, Private Eye, Have I Got News For You*) for automated orchestration across all generator engines in *Flagrants*.

---

## 🎭 The 6 Core Comedy Formulas of Flagrants

### Formula 1: The Pompous Sub-Committee Pedant (Peter Cook / Tony Hancock)
* **Mechanics:** High-register, legalistic, bureaucratic syntax applied to trivial parish disputes.
* **Structural Gap:** High Computational Formalism vs Zero-Value Material (The Triviality).
* **Pattern Template:**
  `[High-Register Municipal Authority Action] + [Pompous Formal Deliberation] + [Absurdly Petty Object/Dispute] + [Deferred Real-World Resolution]`
* **Exemplar:**
  *"The parish council convened an emergency 4-hour meeting to discuss whether a 2012 flyer on the noticeboard should be removed. Decision postponed until 2028."*

---

### Formula 2: The Reluctant Mythic Flight (Spike Milligan / Monty Python)
* **Mechanics:** A legendary or heroic action occurs, but the motivation is completely un-heroic, petty, or weary.
* **Structural Gap:** Mythic History Vector vs Degraded Modern Noun Input.
* **Pattern Template:**
  `[Legendary Figure] + [Iconic Historic Action] + [Hyper-Specific Modern Degraded Scene] + [Deadpan Reluctant Tagline]`
* **Exemplar:**
  *"Robin Hood took one look at Nottingham High Street on a Friday night, saw 40,000 undergraduates drinking 2-for-1 alcopops in the Lace Market, and fled 18 miles into the woods to live in a tree. Who could blame him?"*

---

### Formula 3: The Municipal Lie & Delusional Brochure (Private Eye / HIGNFY)
* **Mechanics:** High-sounding civic marketing jargon applied to brutalist post-war infrastructure disasters.
* **Structural Gap:** Semantic Variance Violation (Ancient Wonder vs 1970s Concrete Car Park).
* **Pattern Template:**
  `[Civic Exhortation ("Experience/Marvel at")] + [1970s Brutalist Facility] + [Hyperbolic Comparison to Ancient Wonders] + [Bleak Commercial Reality]`
* **Exemplar:**
  *"Marvel at the 1974 multi-storey car park ramp, hailed by municipal planners as superior to the Hanging Gardens of Babylon! Features 3 vape outlets and scenic indoor moss growth."*

---

### Formula 4: Hyper-Specific Precision ("Detail is Funny, Generalities are Flat")
* **Mechanics:** Comedy dies in generalities (*"drinking beer"*) and thrives in hyper-specific nouns, numbers, and dates.
* **Rule:** Always specify exact dates, precise numbers, exact vehicle models, and specific brand items.
* **Exemplars:**
  - ❌ General: *"A guy lost his car on the roundabout."*
  - ✅ Specific: *"Spent 4 hours stuck on the 1968 7-tier roundabout in a 2004 Vauxhall Corsa while satellite navigation suggested walking back through the precinct."*

---

### Formula 5: The Antiquity Reachback & Total Tumbleweed
* **Mechanics:** When auditing a town's recent events, reach back centuries to prove modern irrelevance or declare total tumbleweed with pop-culture reluctance.
* **Pattern Template:**
  `[Zero News Declaration] + [Absurd Antiquity Event OR Pop-Culture Reluctance]`
* **Exemplars:**
  - *"Zero breaking news reported in Peacehaven in the last 72 hours. The most significant recent incident on record remains the 1348 Black Death poultry quarantine."*
  - *"RECENT EVENTS: Nada. Zero. Absolutely nothing. Move along, these aren't the droids you're looking for. A parish dog barked at a wheelie bin at 3:14pm, but morale quickly recovered."*

---

### Formula 6: The Laminated Note & Surveillance Matrix
* **Mechanics:** Contrast public order claims with hyper-local passive-aggressive community communications.
* **Pattern Template:**
  `[Low-Tech Gossip Medium (Laminated Note, Nextdoor, Window Notice)] + [Specific Stolen Item / Minor Parking Offence] + [Escalating Threat of Magistrate Action]`
* **Exemplars:**
  - *"Laminated note taped to parish lamp-post: To whoever took my 1970s tupperware box, return it before dusk or the police will be informed."*
  - *"Passive-aggressive note under windshield wiper: You parked 4 inches over my dropped kerb while retrieving a broken shopping trolley."*

---

### Formula 7: The Conversational Empathy Hinge ("...and who could blame them?")
* **Mechanics:** Insert a 3-to-5-word deadpan conversational agreement clause right between a mythic/historic premise and a absurd modern reality.
* **Function:** Transforms a flat factual contrast into an empathetic, weary, human connection.
* **Exemplar:**
  *"Robin Hood took one look at Nottingham High Street on a Friday night, saw 40,000 undergraduates drinking 2-for-1 alcopops in the Lace Market, and fled 18 miles into the woods to live in a tree. **Who could blame him?**"*

---

### Formula 8: Furious Pedantic Indignation (The David Mitchell Voice)
* **Mechanics:** Extremely formal, hyper-articulate legalistic rage applied to the breakdown of minor social rules or municipal logic.
* **Core Mechanism:** Treat a minor social infraction (*queue cutting, wrong cheese brand, car park ramp heights*) with the apocalyptic anger of a Victorian barrister.
* **Exemplar:**
  *"Why can’t everyone just follow the basic rules of the parish 7-tier orbital roundabout system without turning it into a bloodbath?!"*

---

### Formula 9: Detached Analytical Weariness (The Richard Ayoade Voice)
* **Mechanics:** Ultra-logical, elevated vocabulary used to describe complete social discomfort or mundane municipal failure with museum-curator detachment.
* **Core Mechanism:** Express deep emotional weariness through precise, elevated, almost robotic vocabulary.
* **Exemplar:**
  *"I have inspected the precinct. It contains three vape shops and scenic indoor moss. What a ludicrous municipal display."*

---

### Formula 10: Establishment Pomp Collapsing into Chaos (The Monty Python Voice)
* **Mechanics:** The stiff-upper-lip British establishment (*civil servants, academics, army officers*) pretending everything is normal while surrounded by total operational chaos.
* **Core Mechanism:** Demand formal customer service or protocol while standing in a completely dysfunctional environment.
* **Exemplar:**
  *"The Venerable Synod of 731 AD strictly forbade such shenanigans past Vespers, yet here we are standing in a disused bus shelter eating lukewarm scampi."*

---

### Formula 11: The Affectionate Piss-Take & Gallows Humour Principle (Gervais / Boyle)
* **Mechanics:** Self-deprecation first, followed by unfiltered, affectionate piss-taking of regional absurdities.
* **Philosophy:** Mockery as the highest form of intimacy and regional love. Roasting a town's 300-year-old cider contests, 1920s land raffles, or council debt is the ultimate tribute to its authentic British identity.
* **Exemplar:**
  *"We take the piss out of Peacehaven not because we hate it, but because zero degrees longitude passing through a 1916 newspaper raffle town is the most magnificent thing in Sussex."*

2. **Shift Seeds on Every Click:** Use `(hashTown(location, seed) + window._clickCount)` to ensure successive clicks rotate across fresh objects, disputes, recent events, and luminary quotes.
3. **Keep Generic Monoculture <10%:** Never allow generic suburban tropes (*bypasses, kebabs, multi-storeys*) to override authentic town lore unless specifically relevant to that location profile.
