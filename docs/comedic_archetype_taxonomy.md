## 🎛️ Sub-Characteristic Mechanics Matrix

### 1. Sub-Type 2A — Bill Hicks (*Broken Optimism & Transcendent Heartbreak*)
* **Mechanism:** Starts with high, noble expectations for human potential, before collapsing in heartbreak and soaring into transcendent, righteous rage.

### 2. Sub-Type 2B — George Carlin (*Pure Misanthropic Fury*)
* **Mechanism:** Clinical, ferocious disdain observing the sheer, unvarnished circus of collective human stupidity.

### 3. Sub-Type 3A — Ricky Gervais (*Theological & Existential Absurdity*)
* **Mechanism:** Painful domestic cringe combined with mocking divine creation vs local council delays (*"God made the universe in six days; the 1974 planning committee took forty years on the bypass"*).

### 4. Sub-Type 4A — Bill Bailey (*Musical & Lyrical Cadences*)
* **Mechanism:** Sea-shanty rhythms, doom-metal organ refrains, and botanical/zoological absurdity applied to municipal planning (*e.g., an aggressive owl in a tweed jacket observing the ring road*).

### 5. Sub-Type 1A — Richard Ayoade (*Detached Tourist Precision*)
* **Mechanism:** Hyper-articulate, deadpan, overly formal linguistic precision paired with complete emotional emptiness and cynical tourist detachment.

### 6. Sub-Type 1B — David Mitchell (*Pedantic Municipal Rant*)
* **Mechanism:** Acidic, hyper-articulate rage at mild municipal inconveniences and absurd administrative rules.
